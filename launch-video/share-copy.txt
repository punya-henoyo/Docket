Most scanners hand you a queue of maybes, and somebody spends their afternoon deciding which ones
are real. The triage costs more than the scan.

docket files a vulnerability only once it has reproduced it — the literal request it sent, the
literal response it got back — and the refusal is enforced by the type system against its own
agents. No "likely" severity. No confidence score. No percentage, and no score field to compute
one from.

A finding is a reproduction or it does not exist. 2:32.
